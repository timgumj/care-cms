# Third-party software and provenance

The CMS is an independent derivative of reusable custom application code. Client content, assets, operational configuration and database exports are excluded. Symfony skeleton's original MIT notice is retained in LICENSE. The UI integrates EasyAdmin rather than reimplementing its framework; visual editing integrates GrapesJS.

Server dependencies and their exact versions/licenses: composer.lock and vendor/composer/installed.json after installation. Composer installs vendor assets; vendor/ is excluded from the distributable. Self-hosted GrapesJS includes its BSD-3-Clause LICENSE in public/assets/vendor/grapesjs/. Geist and Inconsolata fonts retain their SIL Open Font License notices in public/assets/fonts/.

No copied client frontend, Google Maps imagery or client photography is distributed. Demo HTML, CSS and sample content are fictional. Before publishing a fork, review license obligations for any additional assets/dependencies and preserve their notices.

Original starter demo photographs were generated for this project; see docs/GENERATED-ASSETS.md for provenance and prompts. Uploaded custom fonts must have appropriate rights and are not included in the source distribution.

Inter variable font: Rasmus Andersson / Inter contributors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/rsms/inter . Bundled locally with Inter-OFL.txt; no external font requests.
